The Operational Scenario

The Bottleneck (Current State)

Kamppi Mall Elevator Error 402[cite: 1]

  • A critical fault triggers an alert, requiring a dispatcher to manually cross-reference IoT telemetry, SAP inventory, and Salesforce schedules[cite: 1].
  • This manual data gathering consumes up to 60 minutes before a technician is even dispatched, extending total downtime to 5 hours[cite: 1].
  • Business Risk: Violated service level agreements (SLAs) and constrained operational capacity[cite: 1].

The Target State

Agentic AI via ChatOps (MS Teams)[cite: 1]

  • An AI securely queries these systems simultaneously, pushing a complete triage summary to Microsoft Teams in under 30 seconds[cite: 1].
  • The dispatcher clicks "Approve" to deploy the tech instantly, cutting the end-to-end incident time down to 4 hours[cite: 1].
  • Business Impact: Dispatchers can focus on critical decision-making rather than data hunting, driving a verifiable ~20% operational efficiency gain across the fleet[cite: 1].
"Our architectural priority is enterprise safety: we overlay AI orchestration using read-only adapters, delivering automation with zero risk to your core SAP and Salesforce environments."[cite: 1]

Technical Due Diligence

Integration & API Topology

  • Connectivity: Audit existing SAP and Salesforce endpoints to ensure smooth, secure access[cite: 1].
  • Translation: Determine if we need lightweight middleware to translate legacy ERP data for the AI[cite: 1].
  • Performance: Validate response times so we hit that 30-second triage target, even during morning peak hours[cite: 1].

IoT Telemetry & Edge Latency

  • Data Flow: Map how your elevators transmit faults- whether streaming live via Azure Event Hubs or in batches[cite: 1].
  • Signal Clarity: Identify where noise filtering happens (at the edge or in the cloud) to feed the AI actionable alerts[cite: 1].
  • Standardization: Unify sensor payloads so the AI never hallucinates over dirty data formats[cite: 1].

Security & Access Guardrails

  • Zero Trust: Lock down the AI with strict access controls, granting it strictly read-only access to your systems[cite: 1].
  • Human Authority: Keep your dispatchers in the driver's seat by mandating 1-click approvals for all parts orders over €500[cite: 1].

Compliance & Data Governance

  • Privacy First: Automatically mask technician GPS and shift data in the cloud to guarantee GDPR compliance[cite: 1].
  • Future-Proofing: Build transparent audit trails from day one to align with incoming EU AI Act standards[cite: 1].
  • Encryption: Secure every internal system-to-agent request with enterprise-grade mutual TLS (mTLS)[cite: 1].
